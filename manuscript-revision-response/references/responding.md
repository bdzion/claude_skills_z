# Writing responses to reviewers

## Shape of a response
1. **Answer first.** The first sentence says what was done: "We agree and have added …",
   "We have clarified …", "We respectfully disagree, because …".
2. **What changed and where.** Section, page and lines in the revised manuscript.
3. **The revised text,** quoted, so the reviewer need not search for it.
4. **Brief reasoning** only where the change needs explaining; keep it to what answers the concern.

## Tone
- Thank the editor and reviewers once, in the opening paragraph, not in every response.
- Neutral and specific; no "the reviewer misunderstood". If the reviewer misread, the text
  was probably unclear: clarify the text and say so.
- Treat each reviewer's numbering as theirs; refer to other reviewers' comments by ID when
  answers overlap ("see also our response to R2.4").

## Disagreeing
- Acknowledge the point, give the evidence (data, analysis, citation), and say what you
  changed anyway (often a sentence acknowledging the limitation).
- For requested analyses you will not run, explain why (scope, data availability,
  identifiability) and what the manuscript now says about it.
- Never claim a change that was not made, and never promise future work as if it were done.

## Common requests in watershed and food-system papers
- **Calibration and validation:** periods, time step, metrics with ratings and their
  limits, multi-site or multi-variable checks (see the watershed-model-calibration skill).
- **Uncertainty:** parameter uncertainty bands, scenario ranges, climate-model spread.
- **Scale and transferability:** say what transfers to other watersheds and what does not.
- **Novelty and positioning:** state the gap in one sentence and cite the closest prior work.
- **Policy relevance:** tie results to the decision they inform (targets, programs,
  conservation-authority practice) without overstating.

## Before sending
- [ ] Every comment has a response; no `[RESPONSE PENDING]` left.
- [ ] Every "changes" location matches the final tracked manuscript.
- [ ] Quoted revised text matches the manuscript word for word.
- [ ] Numbers in responses match the manuscript and their sources.
- [ ] New references checked (DOI resolves to the cited work) and added to the reference list.
- [ ] Spelling follows the journal's style (Canadian spelling otherwise).
