# Poster guide

## Narrative
- **Takeaway first.** Write the conclusion headline before anything else: one sentence a
  passer-by can repeat ("Placing cover crops on the 20 % of fields that export most
  phosphorus achieved most of the reduction of whole-watershed adoption" is the form;
  use the actual result).
- **Sections (3–5):** background and gap; study area and data; approach; results;
  implications or next steps. Merge or drop sections rather than shrinking type.
- **One message per section,** stated in its first sentence; the rest supports it.
- **Results carry numbers** with units, period and uncertainty, copied from the manuscript
  (never recomputed or rounded differently on the poster).
- **Mark the status of each claim**: measured, modelled, scenario, or planned work.

## Layout
- Fixed margins, a two- to four-column grid and one gutter width, computed rather than
  placed by eye (the build script does this).
- Reading order: left to right by column, top to bottom within a column; number sections
  if the order is not obvious.
- Large figures for the main result; tables only when exact values matter; group a table
  beside the figure it explains.
- A full-width conclusion band near the bottom, visually distinct from the sections.
- Leave white space; a full poster is not a better poster. Aim for columns of similar fill
  (the build report gives fill per column).

## Figures and maps on posters
- Redraw at poster scale: axis labels at least 24 pt at A0, lines at least 2 pt, markers large.
- At least 150 dpi at printed size for raster images (a figure printed 10 in wide needs
  1500 px or more); vector (PDF, SVG, EMF) preferred.
- Maps keep their scale bar, CRS note and data credits, as in the paper.

## Final checklist
- [ ] Takeaway readable from 2 m (headline size, contrast).
- [ ] Every number matches the manuscript; units on every value and axis.
- [ ] Claims marked as measured, modelled, scenario or planned.
- [ ] No text overflowing or clipped in the render; no body text under 24 pt at A0.
- [ ] Images at or above 150 dpi at print size; logos current and correctly proportioned.
- [ ] Acronyms defined once; Canadian spelling unless the venue template differs.
- [ ] Authors, affiliations, contact, funding acknowledgements (e.g. Mitacs and partner
      logos where required by the funder) and poster ID present.
- [ ] PDF exported from PowerPoint and checked at 100 % before sending to the printer.
