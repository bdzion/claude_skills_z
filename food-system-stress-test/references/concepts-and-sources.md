# Concepts and verified sources

| Concept | Working definition to use | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Food-system resilience | Capacity over time of a food system and its units at multiple levels to provide sufficient, appropriate and accessible food to all in the face of disturbances | Tendall et al. 2015 |
| Vulnerability via history | Past crises show which combinations of ecological fragility, economic exposure and weak institutions turn shocks into famines | Fraser 2007 |
| Fragility of trade networks | Concentration and interdependence in global trade raise exposure to propagating shocks | Puma et al. 2015 |
| Shock frequency | Production shocks across crops, livestock, fisheries and aquaculture have become more frequent | Cottrell et al. 2019 |
| Supply-chain resilience | Resilience depends on diversity, redundancy, buffers and connectivity across supply-chain stages | Davis et al. 2021 |
| Local food-system resilience | Absorptive, adaptive and transformative capacities, and links to food security | Béné 2020 |
| Anticipatory action | Pre-agreed actions released when a forecast or signal crosses a threshold, before impacts | Coughlan de Perez et al. 2015 |

Use the cited paper's own wording when quoting a definition, and check the page.
